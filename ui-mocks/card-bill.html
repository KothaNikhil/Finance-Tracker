Linking a bank card-bill payment to an imported credit-card statement, plus attaching existing transactions to a loan. Source: mobile/src/components/card-bill-sheet.tsx, card-bill-model.ts, attach-existing-sheet.tsx, transaction-detail-controller.tsx, app/lending.tsx. The Card bill sheet opens from the transaction detail’s card-bill action; the detail sheet is hidden while it is open, so the sheet sits directly over whichever tab was showing (Activity here). Attach existing opens from a loan’s detail on Lending, which is likewise hidden. Both are BottomSheets (Modals) that cover the tab bar. Underlying screens are simplified.
